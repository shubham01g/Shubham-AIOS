# Opportunity score (0–100): signals a business needs you

Paste this into the Lead Finder plan prompt, or let Claude adapt it to your offer.

| Signal | Points | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| No website | +30 | They're invisible online, so a site or system is an easy win |
| Website exists but outdated / not mobile-friendly | +15 | Clear, visible problem to point at |
| Google rating under 4.2 | +15 | Reviews often name the exact problem (slow replies, missed calls) |
| Fewer than 30 reviews | +10 | Small, owner-run, so fast decisions |
| Reviews mention "no answer", "never called back", "hard to book" | +20 | A problem automation fixes directly |
| Open less than 6 days a week | +5 | Leads arrive when nobody's there |
| 4.8+ rating and 500+ reviews | −30 | Probably already well served, so skip |

**Score bands:** 70+ = contact first · 40–69 = research more · under 40 = skip for now.

Adjust the signals to what YOU sell. A website agency and an AI receptionist offer score differently.
