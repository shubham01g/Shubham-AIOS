# Discovery call: Dr. Smith Family Dental (DEMO, not a real client)

**Date:** [demo] · **Length:** 25 min · **Who:** Dr. Sarah Smith (owner), Jen (front desk lead)
**Location:** Austin, TX · 2 dentists, 1 hygienist, 2 front desk staff

## What they said (their words)
- "We miss a lot of calls after 5pm and at lunch. Those people just book somewhere else."
- "Jen spends most of her morning calling people back and chasing confirmations."
- "Our Google reviews say nobody picks up. That hurts."
- "We tried an answering service. It was expensive and they got the details wrong."

## Numbers they gave
- Around 15 missed calls a week (Jen's estimate)
- Jen spends about 6 hours a week on call-backs and confirmations
- Front desk cost: about $25/hour
- A new patient is worth roughly $300 in the first year (cleaning + X-ray + exam)
- They think they lose "at least 2 new patients a month" to missed calls

## Tools they use
- Practice management software for bookings
- Gmail for email, Google Business Profile
- No CRM

## What they want
- Every missed call gets a text back within 2 minutes with a booking link
- Automatic appointment confirmations and reminders
- A weekly summary: missed calls, call-backs, bookings recovered

## Constraints
- Budget: "a couple of thousand to start, not a big monthly bill"
- Must not touch patient medical records
- Want it live before the January rush

## Decision
Dr. Smith decides. Wants a proposal by Friday.
